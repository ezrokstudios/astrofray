Tactical Combat Depth

High-Speed Evasion & Tactical Survival

Engineered around aggressive positioning, deterministic physics, and high-risk salvage economics. Direct 360° thruster evasion with full controller and keyboard support.

☄

Physics Asteroids as Dynamic Cover

Drifting asteroid clusters feature authentic 2D rigid-body kinematics. Maneuver behind dense rocks to absorb relentless bullet-hell salvos. Under heavy ballistic pressure, large asteroids fracture into smaller shards—or get punched straight through by penetrating plasma rounds.

Kinetic Deflection Dynamic Fracture Plasma Piercing
☠

Multi-Phase Dreadnought Bosses

Face gargantuan command vessels and alien horrors like the Brood Queen across 10 escalating campaign waves. Bosses cannot simply be burned down: systematically eliminate perimeter point-defense turrets before breaching the exposed reactor core.

Hierarchical Hitboxes Turret Destruction Core Overload
⚙

The 33% Scrap Tax (Greed vs. Safety)

Defeated enemies detonate into glowing Scrap in the heart of the crossfire. Scoop up drops actively during combat for full 100% value, or play it safe and wait for the wave-end auto-vacuum—which claims a 33% salvage tax, granting only 2/3 value.

Realtime Salvage (100%) High-Risk Incentive Auto-Vacuum (2/3 Value)
⚡

Modular Arsenal & Weapon Merging

Automated weaponry fires continuously with predictive targeting. Purchase and merge duplicate weapons in the Upgrade Shop (MK1 → MK2 → MK3) across three distinct weapon families: high-DPS Machine Guns, versatile Ion Blasters, and asteroid-piercing Plasma Cannons. Lock favorite shop cards to save them across rerolls.

Predictive Autofire MK1 → MK3 Fusion Shop Card Locking
Hull Doctrine

Operational Ship Classes

Choose a chassis suited to your survival doctrine—from ultra-maneuverable hit-and-run interceptors to heavily armored weapon platforms.

Interceptor

Ultra-Agile Skirmisher

Relies on blistering acceleration and 3 rapid thruster dashes with 0.25s invulnerability frames (i-frames) to thread the needle through dense bullet curtains.

Scout Fighter

Tactical Fleet All-Rounder

Versatile operational profile with balanced thrusters and 3 hardpoints, accommodating flexible combinations of ballistic and energy weaponry with 2 dash charges.

Heavy Fighter

Armored Gunship

A 5-mount heavy gunship featuring fortified hull plating with built-in damage reduction and an emergency dash, built to anchor the line against overwhelming swarms.
