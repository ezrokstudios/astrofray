Tactical Combat Depth

High-Speed Evasion & Tactical Survival

Engineered around aggressive positioning, deterministic physics, and high-risk salvage economics. Direct 360° thruster evasion with full controller and keyboard support.

☄

Living Debris: Asteroids as Kinetic Shields

Drifting asteroids aren't just hazards—they're your dynamic cover. Duck behind dense rocks to soak up lethal bullet-hell salvos. Under heavy fire, massive asteroids fracture into jagged shrapnel, while penetrating plasma blasts punch straight through to hostiles on the other side.

Kinetic Shielding Destructive Fracture Plasma Piercing
☠

Multi-Phase Dreadnought Encounters

Confront colossal flagships and cosmic horrors across escalating 10-wave expeditions. Bosses aren't simple damage sponges: systematically strip their point-defense turrets, evade secondary bullet phases, and breach their exposed reactor core before you're overwhelmed.

Targetable Turrets Escalating Phases Reactor Overload
⚙

The 33% Salvage Tax (Risk vs. Reward)

Destroyed enemies detonate into glowing Scrap directly in the crossfire. Scoop up salvage in the heat of combat for 100% yield, or play it safe and rely on the wave-end auto-vacuum—which claims a 33% salvage tax. Greed pays, but only if you survive.

Live Salvage (100%) High-Stakes Greed Auto-Vacuum (2/3 Yield)
⚡

Modular Weapon Fusion (MK1 → MK3)

Onboard weapon batteries track and lead hostiles with automated predictive targeting. Buy and fuse matching weapons between waves to evolve them from MK1 to MK3 tiers. Forge lethal synergies across rapid-fire ballistics, crowd-controlling ion blasters, and asteroid-splitting plasma cannons.

Hull Doctrine

Operational Ship Classes

Choose a chassis suited to your survival doctrine—from ultra-maneuverable hit-and-run interceptors to heavily armored weapon platforms.

Interceptor

Ultra-Agile Skirmisher

Built for pure twitch evasion. Features blistering acceleration and 3 rapid thruster dashes (0.25s i-frames) to thread the needle through dense bullet curtains.

Scout Fighter

Tactical Fleet All-Rounder

The adaptable frontline backbone. Combines balanced 360° thrusters with 3 hardpoints to field flexible combinations of ballistic and energy weaponry.

Heavy Fighter

Armored Gunship

A 5-hardpoint flying fortress. Reinforced hull plating shrugs off grazing fire with passive damage reduction, built to anchor the line against overwhelming swarms.
