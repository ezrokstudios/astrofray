Engineered around aggressive positioning, deterministic physics, and high-risk salvage economics.

☄

Physics Asteroids as Cover

Drifting asteroid clusters feature authentic 2D rigid-body kinematics. Maneuver behind dense rocks to absorb relentless bullet-hell salvos. Under heavy ballistic pressure, large asteroids fracture into smaller shards—or get punched straight through by penetrating plasma rounds.

Kinetic Deflection Dynamic Fracture Plasma Piercing
☠

Multi-Phase Dreadnought Bosses

Face gargantuan command vessels and alien horrors like the Brood Queen across the 10 campaign waves. Bosses cannot simply be burned down: systematically eliminate perimeter point-defense turrets and shield generators before breaching the exposed reactor core.

Hierarchical Hitboxes Turret Destruction Core Overload
⚙

The 33% Scrap Tax (Greed vs. Safety)

Defeated enemies detonate into glowing Scrap in the middle of active crossfire. Collect drops actively during combat for 100% value, or play safely and wait for the wave-end auto-vacuum—which deducts a 33% recovery tax (granting only 2/3 value).

Realtime Salvage (100%) Greed Incentives Auto-Vacuum (2/3 Value)
⚡

Modular Arsenal & Weapon Merging

Automated weaponry fires continuously based on predictive targeting. Purchase and merge duplicate weapons in the Upgrade Shop (MK1 → MK2 → MK3) across three distinct weapon families: rapid-fire Machine Guns, chain-disrupting Ion Blasters, and armor-penetrating Plasma Cannons. Lock favorite shop cards to save them across rerolls.

Predictive Autofire MK1 → MK3 Fusion Shop Card Locking
Hull Doctrine

Operational Ship Classes

Choose a chassis suited to your survival doctrine—from ultra-maneuverable hit-and-run interceptors to heavily armored weapon platforms.

Interceptor

Ultra-Agile Skirmisher

Relies on lightning-fast acceleration and 3 rapid thruster dashes with 0.25s invulnerability frames (i-frames) to thread the needle through dense bullet curtains.

Scout Fighter

Balanced Fleet Scout

Versatile operational profile with balanced thrusters and hardpoints, accommodating flexible combinations of ballistic and energy weaponry with 2 dash charges.

Heavy Fighter

Armored Dreadnought

A 5-mount heavy gunship featuring fortified hull plating with built-in damage reduction and an emergency dash, built to anchor the line against overwhelming swarms.
